# Market Insights Tracker

**Tech:** Python · requests · BeautifulSoup · TextBlob · pandas · matplotlib

## What it does
A modular pipeline for monitoring competitor prices, consumer sentiment,
and emerging market trends from public sources.

## Modules
- Web scraper for competitor pricing data (configurable targets)
- TextBlob sentiment analysis on product reviews
- Anomaly detection using z-score on price time series
- Moving average trend tracking with spike/drop alerts
- Keyword trend extraction from review text
- Share of voice calculation across brands
- Price benchmarking — own vs market average
